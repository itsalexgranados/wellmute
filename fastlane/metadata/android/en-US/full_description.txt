<p>A free, open source alternative to Digital Wellbeing for GrapheneOS and any Android without Google services.</p>
<ul>
<li>Dashboard with today's screen time, unlocks and notifications</li>
<li>Daily app timers that pause apps until midnight</li>
<li>Focus mode and Bedtime mode</li>
<li>Custom system modes with per-app notification filtering</li>
</ul>
<p>No internet permission. No analytics. All data stays on your device.</p>
